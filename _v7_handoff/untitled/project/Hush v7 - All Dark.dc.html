13 — WHEN SOMETHING HURTS
Say it once. The plan bends around you.
Pain isn’t a failure to log — it’s information. A quiet affordance lives in the Paused screen; reporting it isn’t buried, but it never shouts either. The engine responds in real time: it drops the load, or swaps in a safer movement that trains the same muscle. No doctor cosplay, no alarm. Just a coach adjusting.
