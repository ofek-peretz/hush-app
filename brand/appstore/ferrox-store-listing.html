הדמות מראה, לא מתארת

FERROX is a personal gym coach that writes your week and stands with you through every set.

A week written for you
How many days you have, what your gym has, what you're after and what hurts. From that comes a full week: named, with days and lifts that fit your equipment. Swap a lift, drag to reorder, or get a new week when something changes.

The figure shows, it doesn't describe
Every lift is shown on a 3D figure moving at the right tempo, depth and range. During the workout it does the set with you, so there's nothing to read between reps.

Your phone stays in your pocket
With earbuds in, the coach asks when you're ready, counts your rest and logs your reps from what you say. Prefer to tap? The same workout lives on your lock screen, your Apple Watch and in the app, with one rest clock shared by all of them.

Every set is kept
Every weight, every rep and every week, so next week starts from what you've already done. At the end of the week, a summary of what changed.

Runs and walks
Distance, pace and heart rate with GPS outdoors, or steps on the treadmill.

Apple Health
Heart rate is read from Health, and finished workouts are saved to it.

FERROX Pro
Your first 3 workouts are free, no card needed, and then 14 more days free. After that, FERROX Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, priced in the app in your currency. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account, and the subscription renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.
